# Alessandro Leanza

PhD Candidate in AI & Robotics at IDSIA USI-SUPSI.

I work on embodied AI and robot learning, with a focus on connecting reasoning, perception, and physical interaction.

My research includes:
- knowledge-grounded LLM and RAG systems for robot planning;
- multimodal robot learning from vision, force, and contextual signals;
- diffusion policies and compliant manipulation;
- adaptive human–robot interaction and wearable robotics.

I am interested in research and engineering roles at the intersection of machine learning, embodied AI, and robotics.

## Selected work

- **[ConceptBot](https://github.com/alessandro-leanza/ConceptBot)** — Knowledge-graph-grounded commonsense reasoning for robot task planning.
- **[Graph-Based RAG for Robot Planning](https://github.com/alessandro-leanza/Graph-Based-RAG_RobotPlanning)** — Provenance-aware retrieval with local LLMs and persistent knowledge graphs.
- **[Context-Aware Exoskeleton Assistance](https://github.com/alessandro-leanza/Exoskeleton)** — Gaze, vision, and adaptive control for wearable robotic assistance.
- **[Learning Compliance with Diffusion Policies](https://alessandro-leanza.github.io/projects/diffusion-compliance/)** — Learning motion and compliance schedules for contact-rich manipulation.

## Technical focus

Python · PyTorch · ROS 2 · Docker · LLM/RAG · Knowledge Graphs · Diffusion Policies · Computer Vision · Force / Compliance Control
